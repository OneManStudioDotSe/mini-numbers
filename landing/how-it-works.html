Get started

How it works

Mini Numbers is one small program you run on your own server. Your site tells it "someone looked at this page", and it keeps count. That's the whole trick.

1. Start it

One command. The database is built in, so there is nothing else to install.

What comes after the command is in the setup guide.

2. Answer six questions

Open your new address and a setup wizard greets you. It asks for a password, a privacy mode and not much else. No config files, no restart.

In a hurry? Quick Setup picks sensible answers and hands you a strong password.

3. Paste one script tag

Create a project in the dashboard and it gives you a snippet. Put it on your site:

your-site.html 
<script async
  src="https://stats.example.com/tracker/tracker.min.js"
  data-project-key="YOUR_PROJECT_KEY"></script>
  • 2 KB gzipped. Your hero image is bigger.
  • No cookies. Nothing to ask consent for.
  • Single-page apps just work.
  • Custom events are one line: MiniNumbers.track("signup")
  • Polite. Browsers that send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control aren't counted.

4. Look at your numbers

Open /admin-panel. Page views, visitors, where they came from, what they read, where they gave up. First visits show up within seconds.

What about privacy?

Mini Numbers never stores an IP address. It turns each visitor into a scrambled ID instead:

The whole formula 
visitorId = SHA-256(ip + browser + site + secret + time window)

The time window is the clever part. When it rolls over (every 24 hours unless you choose otherwise), yesterday's visitor becomes a stranger, so nobody can be followed for long. The full story is in the privacy notes.

Who built this?

One human, with a lot of help from an AI. The AI wrote much of the code and all of the confident comments. The human said "no" a lot. There are 450 automated tests because neither fully trusts the other.
