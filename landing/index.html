Your week, at a glance

Open it, look, close it. Seven numbers tell you how things went, each with its change from the period before.

  • Page views, visitors, sessions, bounce rate
  • This period against the last one
  • Where people came from, AI assistants included
  • Browsers, systems, devices and countries
  • Light and dark, because of course

What they read, and how they found it

Your most-read pages, where visits start and end, and which links, searches and campaigns sent people over.

  • Top pages, entry pages and exit pages
  • Referrers, with AI assistants called out
  • Campaigns: UTM source, medium and campaign
  • Narrow any chart to one browser, device, country or referrer

  • Scroll depth, so you know who made it to the end

  • Countries, then cities, on a globe

Privacy isn't a setting here. It's how the thing is built.

Your visitors' data stays on your server. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and nobody gets followed around the internet.

visitorHash = SHA256(
  ip + userAgent + projectId
  + serverSalt + rotationBucket
)

No cookies

Nothing is left on your visitor's device beyond a visit ID that dies with the tab. No cookie, no cookie banner.

No stored IP addresses

An address lives in memory for one request, then it's gone. It never touches the disk.

Forgetful by design

Visitor IDs rotate every 1 to 8,760 hours. You choose how soon yesterday's visitor becomes a stranger.

No guessing

A chip in the dashboard says, in plain words, exactly what your server keeps about a visitor.

Pick your way in

Three ways to start it. They all end at the same setup wizard.

Terminal 
# In an empty folder on a server with Docker. Use your own domain.
curl -fsSLO \
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OneManStudioDotSe/mini-numbers/main/docker-compose.yml
echo "DOMAIN=stats.example.com" > .env
docker compose up -d

# Open https://stats.example.com: HTTPS is set up for you
Terminal 
# For a look around. Needs Docker.
docker run --rm -p 8080:8080 -v mini-numbers-data:/app/data ghcr.io/onemanstudiodotse/mini-numbers:latest

# Open http://localhost:8080
Terminal 
# Needs Java 21. Download mini-numbers.jar from the latest release on GitHub, then:
java -jar mini-numbers.jar

# Open http://localhost:8080

The setup wizard asks six questions and starts everything without a restart. In a hurry? Quick Setup answers them for you.
