Privacy isn't a setting here. It's how the thing is built.

Your visitors' data stays on your server. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and nobody gets followed around the internet.

No cookies

Nothing is left on your visitor's device beyond a visit ID that dies with the tab. No cookie, no cookie banner.

No stored IP addresses

An address lives in memory for one request, then it's gone. It never touches the disk.

Forgetful by design

Visitor IDs rotate every 1 to 8,760 hours. You choose how soon yesterday's visitor becomes a stranger.

No guessing

A chip in the dashboard says, in plain words, exactly what your server keeps about a visitor.

How a visitor is counted

With no cookie to lean on, each visitor becomes a scrambled ID. The IP address goes into the sum and is thrown away; only the result is stored, and it can't be turned back.

The whole formula 
visitorId = SHA-256(
  ip + browser + site
  + secret + time window
)

When the time window rolls over, the same person gets a brand new ID, so nobody can be followed for long.
