Plain words first. The formal version is the MIT licence, and if the two ever disagree, the licence wins.

What you get

Mini Numbers is free, open-source software that you run yourself. There's no hosted service, no account with us and no bill. Nothing is held back for a paid plan, because there isn't one.

  • Ready to use: the dashboard, goals, funnels, segments, CSV export, the API, and as many sites and users as you like.
  • Labs: revenue, webhooks, email reports and embeddable widgets are off until you switch them on. They work, but they may still change between releases.
  • On the way: support for Android, iOS and Flutter apps. Today Mini Numbers counts websites.

What you can do with it

  • Use it for anything, commercial work included.
  • Change it and share your version.
  • Build it into your own product.

Just keep the licence notice with it.

What's on you

  • Your server: keeping it running, updated and secure.
  • Your visitors: following the privacy laws that apply to you, and choosing the privacy mode and retention that fit.
  • Your data: it lives on your server. We can't see it, and we can't restore it. Make backups.
  • What you send in: the properties of a custom event are stored as you send them, so keep names, email addresses and the like out of them.
  • The labs you switch on: webhooks post to the address you give them, email reports go through your own mail server, and a site with widgets on shows its numbers to anyone who has that site's key.

What's inside from others

Mini Numbers stands on other people's work, each under its own licence:

  • Locations come from GeoLite2 data created by MaxMind, available from maxmind.com.
  • Browsers and devices are recognised with the uap-core rules (Apache 2.0).
  • The typefaces are Plus Jakarta Sans and Inter (SIL Open Font License), and the icons are Remix Icon (Apache 2.0).

No warranty

The software comes as it is, with no warranty of any kind, and its authors aren't liable for what happens when you use it. A fair amount of it was written with an AI and checked by one human and 450 tests. Read the code, test it, and decide for yourself.
