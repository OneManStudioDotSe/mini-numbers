Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version: this website collects nothing about you. It would be awkward if it did.

This website

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics. Not even our own.
  • No forms, no accounts, no email list.
  • Nothing loaded from other companies. Fonts and icons are served from this site.

One thing is remembered: whether you picked the light or dark theme. That stays in your own browser and is never sent anywhere.

Whoever hosts this site may keep ordinary server logs. We don't look at them to learn about you.

The software

Mini Numbers itself runs on the servers of the people who install it. We never see their data, or yours. What it's built to do:

  • No cookies in your browser.
  • No stored IP addresses. An address is used for a moment and thrown away.
  • Visitors are forgotten on a schedule the site owner chooses.
  • Three privacy modes that decide how much is kept at all.

The details are in the privacy notes.

If a site you visit uses Mini Numbers

That site's owner runs it and holds the data, so they're the one to ask. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, Mini Numbers doesn't count you by default.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there's nothing for us to show, fix or delete. Easiest request we'll ever get.

Changes and questions

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions go to the contact page.
